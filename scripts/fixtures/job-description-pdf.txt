Position Summary
We are seeking an AI Engineer to design, build, evaluate, and deploy intelligent systems that improve
products and experiences at scale. The role combines applied machine learning, software engineering,
and rigorous experimentation, with an emphasis on reliable, efficient, and responsible AI solutions. The
engineer will collaborate with research scientists, product teams, infrastructure engineers, and
cross-functional partners to translate complex AI capabilities into measurable product impact.
Key Responsibilities
• Design and implement end-to-end machine learning systems, from data preparation and model
experimentation to deployment and monitoring.
• Build and improve deep learning and generative AI solutions, including large language model (LLM)
applications, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and multimodal experiences where relevant.
• Train, fine-tune, evaluate, and optimize models for accuracy, latency, throughput, reliability, and cost.
• Create robust data pipelines, evaluation datasets, automated tests, model services, and observability
workflows.
• Partner with research, infrastructure, privacy, safety, and product teams to move prototypes into
production.
• Conduct experiments, analyze model behavior and failure modes, and communicate results and trade-offs
clearly.
• Apply security, privacy, fairness, and responsible AI practices throughout the model lifecycle.
Minimum Qualifications
• Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field, or equivalent
practical experience.
• Strong programming experience in Python and proficiency with software engineering fundamentals,
algorithms, and data structures.
• Hands-on experience developing and shipping machine learning or deep learning solutions.
• Working knowledge of modern ML frameworks such as PyTorch and data tools such as SQL.
• Ability to design experiments, interpret offline and online metrics, and troubleshoot production systems.
• Effective collaboration and written and verbal communication skills.
Preferred Qualifications

• Advanced degree in an AI-related discipline or equivalent applied industry experience.
• Experience with transformer architectures, LLM adaptation, embeddings, vector search, RAG, agents, or

• Experience with distributed training or inference, GPU acceleration, model serving, and ML infrastructure.
• Familiarity with ranking, recommendation, natural language processing, computer vision, or large-scale

• Experience with A/B testing, production monitoring, CI/CD, containers, and cloud-native systems.
Technology & Engineering Focus
Core: Python, PyTorch, SQL, Git, APIs, model evaluation, and production-quality engineering.
Role-dependent: Transformers, LLM tooling, vector databases, distributed systems, GPU computing,
orchestration, Docker/Kubernetes, and experiment tracking.
Success Measures
Success in this role is measured by meaningful improvements in model and product quality, dependable
deployment and monitoring, efficient use of computing resources, safe and privacy-conscious design, and
effective execution across teams.
